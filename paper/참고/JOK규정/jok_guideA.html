Guide for Authors


Submission Guidelines

1. Papers submitted to the journal must be related to information science and applications thereof, contribute to academic/industrial developments, and should be original in content. Published work (or work that is under consideration for publication) at other journals, whether domestic or international, may not be submitted. Authors are expected to comply with research and publication guidelines such as Ethical Standards for the Korean Institute of Information Scientists and Engineers and International Standards for Editors and Authors.

2. Authors shall take full responsibility for papers published in the journal.

　

3. The Editorial Board has the right to request revision of papers from the authors. In order to retain status, revisions must be returned within 6 weeks for general papers, 3 weeks for invited contributions and short papers, and 2 weeks for papers requiring 2-month review. Also, if there is no reply from the author, it is considered to quit the revision process.
　

4. Authors must submit their papers to one of the topic areas listed below. Depending on the topic area of an accepted paper, an appropriate journal will be decided. If the editorial board finds that the topic area of the submitted paper needs to be changed, the editorial board may revise the topic area of the submitted paper.
　

Topic Areas

Published Journal

• Computer Systems and Theory

Journal of KIISE(JOK)

hardware, OS, graphics, parallel processing, computing theory, etc.

• Software and Applications

software engineering, artificial intelligence, HCI, PL, natural language processing, computer education, etc.

• Databases

DBMS, information systems management, data mining, information retrieval, XML, geographic information systems, etc.

• Information Networking

wired and wireless networks, mobile communications, internet communications and applications, information security, multimedia communications, sensor networks, etc.

• Computing Practices

KIISE Transactions on Computing Practices(KTCP)

real-world/industrial/academic experiences focused on design and implementation of computer software or computer systems, development of software tools, evaluation and analyses of computing systems and data, etc.


5.
(1) Papers must be submitted online via the official website (https://journal.kiise.or.kr/).
(2) Submissions are accepted year-round, and an acknowledgment from the secretariat establishes the date of receipt(kiise@kiise.or.kr, Tel:+82-2-588-9240).

6. Papers must be submitted in PDF format. If submitted as a non-PDF, 10,000 won will be charged for file conversion. Papers for publication must be prepared in Hangul or MS Word.

7.
(1) Papers should be organized in the following order: Korean title, English title, Korean abstract, Korean key words, English abstract, English key words, main body, references, appendix. Do not include the name(s) of authors or any other identifiable information. (2) Abstracts should be kept to 300~500 characters in Korean, and 100~200 words in English. The number of key words for both Korean and English is limited to 4~6. (3) Short papers must not exceed 5 pages, following the format and style of the journal. (4) Do not include acknowledgements in the review submission papers.

8. References should be related to the papers and numbered consecutively in the order in which they are first mentioned in the text. Journal paper references should include the following: author’s name, title, name of journal, volume, issue, pages, and year of publication. Book references should contain author’s name, publisher, and year of publication. References are in (translated/transliterated) Roman script (recommended but not mandatory).

9. Chapters and sections should be labeled with Arabic numerals i.e. 1., 1.1.

10. Figures and tables should be placed in the center of the page and sufficiently large so that they remain legible when reduced to the print size. Figures should be labeled as Fig. 1, center-aligned below the figure, and tables as Table 1, center-aligned above the table. The content of all in-text figures and tables must be in English, and the titles in both Korean and English.

11. Authors of selected papers must submit a short 200-word biography and a photo (3 x 4 cm).

12. Authors must pay 500,000 won per paper requiring 2-month review, or 50,000 won for general review. Authors of papers requiring 2-month review may receive a refund if review is not completed within 2 months. However, this is not applied the delay is due to the author. 13. Authors who wish to change the date of publication must receive approval from the Editorial Board. Also, an additional charge of 300,000 won applies.

14. Papers published in the journal cannot be reproduced without permission from the Korean Institute of Information Scientists and Engineers.

15. By publishing, authors agree to have their articles stored in electronic databases that are accessible by an unspecified number of the general public. Authors of articles approved for publication are required to submit the Copyright Transfer Form.

16. These guidelines shall take effect as of June 27, 2024.

Authorship

Authorship credit should be based on 1) substantial contributions to conception and design, acquisition of data, or analysis and interpretation of data; 2) drafting the article or revising it critically for important intellectual content; and 3) final approval of the version to be published. Authors should meet these three conditions.

　

　

Peer Review Process

All manuscripts are treated as confidential. They are peer-reviewed by at least three anonymous reviewers selected by the editor. The review process operates under a double-blind system. The corresponding author is notified as soon as possible of the editor’s decision to accept, reject, or request revision of manuscripts. When the final revised manuscript is determined completely acceptable according to the journal format and criteria, it will be scheduled for publication in the next available issue. Rejected papers will not be peer-reviewed again.


The criteria on paper reviewing:
- How does this manuscript advance the field of research and/or contribute something new to the literature?
- Is this manuscript relevant to the community of readers of the journal?
- Is the manuscript technically sound?
- Is the paper clearly written and well organized?
- Are all figures and tables appropriately provided and is their resolution in good quality?
- Does the introduction state the objectives of the manuscript, encouraging the reader to read on?
- Are the references relevant and complete? The reviewers should supply missing references.

　

　

　

Guidelines for Complaints and Appeals

An author who wishes to express a dissenting or different perspective or viewpoint on the reviewer's review comments, revision requests, and reasons for reconsideration may submit a written appeal (a letter or an e-mail) to the Editorial Board on a one-time basis, providing substantial arguments or empirical examples. If the editorial board and the editor-in-chief determines that the appeal is valid, then the editorial board makes the final decision. The author may not appeal the final decision of the Editorial Board again.

　

　

Copyright

By publishing, authors agree to have their articles stored in electronic databases that are accessible by an unspecified number of the general public. Authors of articles approved for publication are required to submit the Copyright Transfer Form. The copyright of the paper is transferred to KIISE when the copyright transfer form is submitted to KIISE.

　

　

Data Sharing and Reproducibility

We promote open science by recommending authors to include data sharing methods in their manuscripts. It is recommended that authors deposit their data, code, and materials in reputable repositories whenever possible. For proprietary or sensitive data, authors may explain access restrictions and provide a process for qualified researchers to request data. Manuscripts should contain sufficient methodological detail to enable replication. By fostering transparency and data sharing, we aim to enhance research credibility, accelerate scientific progress, and maximize the impact of published work.
